Drivel has a website now, which it did not on Tuesday.

It is served from the project repository itself rather than from one of its own,
and the manual you can read under [Docs](/drivel/docs/) is not a copy: those
pages are built from `docs/user/` in the repository, so there is exactly one
version of every instruction and it is the one the maintainers edit. If a page
here disagrees with the repository, the page here is a bug.

What lands here from now on is releases, and whatever the
[hyena](https://github.com/syshlted/drivel/blob/master/docs/project/mascot.md)
has to say for itself.
